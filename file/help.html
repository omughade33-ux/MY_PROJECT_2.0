How do I post a load?

Login to your company account, go to the Post a Load tab, fill in the shipment details, and submit. Verified transporters will start bidding within minutes.

How do I contact a transporter?

Once you accept a transporter's bid, their phone number is revealed and they will contact you directly to confirm the booking.

How is GST verification done?

We validate the GST number format during registration and cross-check it against our verified database. Use the Verify GST page to check any number.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Passwords are hashed with SHA-256 and all API communication uses HTTPS in production. OTP verification secures account creation.

What truck types are supported?

10-Tyre, 12-Tyre, 14-Tyre, Container, and Open Body trucks are supported. You can filter loads by truck type on the browse page.
