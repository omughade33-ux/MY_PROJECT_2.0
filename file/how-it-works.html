How CargoConnect Works

1

Register & Verify

Companies and transporters register with their GST number and phone. OTP verification ensures every account is genuine.

2

Post Your Load

Companies post shipment details — goods type, weight, route, truck type, and pickup date. It goes live instantly.

3

Transporters Bid

Verified transporters browse open loads and place competitive bids with their price and a message.

4

Confirm Booking

The company reviews all bids and accepts the best one. The load status changes to Booked and other bids are closed automatically.

5

Deliver & Complete

The transporter picks up the load and delivers it. The status is updated to In Transit and then Delivered upon completion.
